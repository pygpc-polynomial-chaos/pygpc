What you need to do on Zenodo
=============================
1. Upload the three files under exactly these names: S1.h5, AP.h5, mu_spiketimes_S1.h5.
2. Put the DOI into L30. Use the version DOI of that specific record, not the "all versions" concept DOI.
3. If you ever change a file, the hash in the registry must change too. Run sha256sum on the new file and update
   the matching line.

Other things I noticed

  ReadTheDocs will show as its title.
